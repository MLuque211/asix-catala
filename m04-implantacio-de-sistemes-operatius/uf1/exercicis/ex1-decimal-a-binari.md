## Exercici 1: Conversió de Base 10 a Base 2

Convertir els següents nombres en sistema decimal ($10$) a binari ($2$):

* **a)** $122_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **b)** $343_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **c)** $92_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **d)** $72_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **e)** $45_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **f)** $88_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **g)** $156_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **h)** $201_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **i)** $255_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$
* **j)** $512_{10} \rightarrow ?_2$

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Veure respostes</b></summary>

* **a)** $122_{10} = \mathbf{1111010_2}$
* **b)** $343_{10} = \mathbf{101010111_2}$
* **c)** $92_{10} = \mathbf{1011100_2}$
* **d)** $72_{10} = \mathbf{1001000_2}$
* **e)** $45_{10} = \mathbf{101101_2}$
* **f)** $88_{10} = \mathbf{1011000_2}$
* **g)** $156_{10} = \mathbf{10011100_2}$
* **h)** $201_{10} = \mathbf{11001001_2}$
* **i)** $255_{10} = \mathbf{11111111_2}$
* **j)** $512_{10} = \mathbf{1000000000_2}$
</details>

## Exercici 4: Conversió de Base 2 a Base 16

Convertir els següents nombres en base binària ($2$) a hexadecimal ($16$) agrupant de 4 en 4 bits:

* **a)** $100010_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **b)** $110111001_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **c)** $10110011011_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **d)** $1110011001_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **e)** $1010101010_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **f)** $0101010101_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **g)** $11001100001_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **h)** $11000000011_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **i)** $10000001110_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$
* **j)** $1011111111101_2 \rightarrow ?_{16}$


<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Veure respostes</b></summary>

* **a)** $100010_2 = \mathbf{22_{16}}$
* **b)** $110111001_2 = \mathbf{1B9_{16}}$
* **c)** $10110011011_2 = \mathbf{59B_{16}}$
* **d)** $1110011001_2 = \mathbf{399_{16}}$
* **e)** $1010101010_2 = \mathbf{2AA_{16}}$
* **f)** $0101010101_2 = \mathbf{155_{16}}$
* **g)** $11001100001_2 = \mathbf{661_{16}}$
* **h)** $11000000011_2 = \mathbf{603_{16}}$
* **i)** $10000001110_2 = \mathbf{40E_{16}}$
* **j)** $1011111111101_2 = \mathbf{17FD_{16}}$

</details>
